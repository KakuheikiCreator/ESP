# Alarm
This is the firmware for an intrusion detector.
It is created using Bluetooth LE's message sending and receiving function.
It also uses the nano toolkit framework as a component.

# Release History

| Version | Release Date | Overview |
| ---- | ---- | ---- |

| V1.1 | 2025/07/07 | Miniaturized hardware and support for new hardware (ESP32-C6, etc.) and ESP-IDF V5.4.2 |

# System configuration
This software was developed in the ESP-IDF environment.
The system configuration at the time of development was as follows:

Microcontroller: ESP32
ESP-IDF: V5.3.1 (※V5.0 or later should work.)
nano toolkit framework: V1.0

# Background of the system development
Japan is said to have good public safety, but in reality it is a surveillance society with almost no privacy.

And many people self-deprecatingly say that it is like a colony with no change of government.

This social problem is the main reason why I decided to develop this system.

I had an acquaintance who was a former secretary to a minister who had been politically active for many years.

This was a complete coincidence, as I was not involved in political activities and did not belong to any particular political organization.

Just because I had someone close to me who was politically active, I was monitored by the media and the police, my house was always bugged, and someone would sometimes open the door when I was away.

In addition, I had been suspecting that something had been mixed into the food and drinks in my house, and I had been feeling sick from time to time for quite some time.

In other words, I was a victim of crimes committed by the ruling class and anti-social forces in both the public and private sectors, including the political party of the former secretary to the minister who was close to me.

I can't go into the details of what happened, but to cut straight to the point, after I entered the workforce, I had a detective company investigate the room next door.

The investigation revealed that the room next door was occupied by local juvenile delinquents and police-related members of crime prevention groups, while the room next door was rented by media personnel.

It was a fairly large production company in Tokyo.

The room was always empty, but a mobile phone was emitting signals from it.
In other words, they were using a highly sensitive microphone and a modified mobile phone to eavesdrop on my room from the room next door almost every day.

Moreover, on the day I asked the detective to investigate while I was out, they contacted a real estate company and moved out that same day.

Japanese people move 3 to 4 times in their lifetime.
And the reality is that the TV industry has a population of less than 10,000 people.

If we calculate based on the assumption that people move once every 20 years, the probability of moving on a specific day, that is, the day of the investigation, is 1 in 7,300.
The probability that the target is someone in the TV industry is 1/73,000,000.

In Japan, the probability of winning the lottery is about 1/6,100,000, so this is less than 1/10 of that probability, and is definitely not a coincidence.

Naturally, after this was discovered, the police, media, and political and business circles
held a grudge against me, and my clients would often mix drugs into their coffee and other drinks.

I caught a taxi to the hospital, where I took a urine test, which repeatedly showed the presence of drugs. (I will post a photo of the medical certificate.)

Myself, my family, and the people around me have all been subjected to similar abuse.

Given this background, it was inevitable for me to develop this system.
